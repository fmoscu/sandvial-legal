Sandvial: Liquid Sort Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Sandvial: Liquid Sort Puzzle (“Sandvial”, “the app”) is a casual liquid-sorting puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Florian Moscu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). We do not receive them and we have no user accounts or servers for this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before showing personalised ads we ask for your consent through Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice later from Settings → Ad privacy (shown in those regions; open Settings with the gear in the main menu, or from Pause → Settings), or by reinstalling the app.
  • iOS: if the app asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), you can say Ask App Not to Track and still play; ads then are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records game events (levels started and finished, moves, stars, hints used) together with an app instance identifier, device and app information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. It does not use your device’s advertising identifier, and these statistics are not used to personalise ads.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, statistics run only if you agree in Google’s consent message, and you can change your choice from Settings → Ad privacy. Google processes this data for us: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The app does not include a crash-reporting service.

4. Children

Sandvial is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

We have no accounts and cannot link game statistics to your name or email, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through ads, you can use Google’s tools (myadcenter.google.com). If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you also have the right to object, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
