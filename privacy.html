Privacy Policy · Sandvial: Liquid Sort Puzzle

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Sandvial: Liquid Sort Puzzle (“Sandvial”, “the app”) is a casual liquid-sorting puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Florian Moscu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress, scores and settings are stored on your device (in the app’s local storage). Apart from the game statistics described in section 3, we do not receive them, and we have no user accounts or servers for this data. If you use Google Play Games or Apple Game Center (section 4), a copy of your progress is also kept in your own Google or Apple account. If you delete the app, the copy on your device is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before showing personalised ads we ask for your consent through Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice later from Settings → Ad privacy (shown in those regions; open Settings with the gear in the main menu, or from Pause → Settings), or by reinstalling the app.
  • iOS: if the app asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), you can say Ask App Not to Track and still play; ads then are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads; the path depends on the phone).

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records game events (levels started and finished, worlds completed, moves, stars, hints used) together with an app instance identifier, device and app information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. It does not use your device’s advertising identifier, and these statistics are not used to personalise ads.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, statistics run only if you agree in Google’s consent message, and you can change your choice from Settings → Ad privacy. Google processes this data for us: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The app does not include a crash-reporting service.

4. Achievements, leaderboard and cloud save (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center, iCloud)

On Android the app uses Google Play Games Services, and on iOS Apple Game Center and iCloud, for achievements, a leaderboard of total stars and a copy of your progress in your own account. If you are signed in to Play Games or Game Center, the app sends them the achievements you unlocked, your total number of stars and your game progress (scores per level, daily level streak and which tutorials you have seen). Your player name and your score can be seen by other players on the leaderboard. On iOS, the progress copy is stored in your iCloud account.

These services are run by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies (policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy). We do not receive your name, email or account details. You can play without signing in; you can delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile or Google Account, and manage Game Center and iCloud in your iPhone settings.

5. Children

Sandvial is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

We have no accounts and cannot link game statistics to your name or email, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through ads, you can use Google’s tools (myadcenter.google.com). If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you also have the right to object, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
